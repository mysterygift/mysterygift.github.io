DELIVER

DELIVER

Out the door. On time.

Paperwork is generated from the schedule, not retyped. Then every deliverable is tracked until it is signed off.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Call sheets built from the schedule.
  • Watermarked for every recipient.
  • Every deliverable tracked to sign-off.
  • Wrap with a checklist, not a guess.
01

Call Sheets

Pick a shoot day and unit. Albatross pulls in the schedule, locations, booked cast and crew by department, and the weather forecast. Preview, then save the PDF. If the day's risk assessment is missing or not signed off, it warns you before you export.

02

One per recipient

Export a call sheet for everyone on the day, each one watermarked with the recipient's name.

03

Movement Orders

Generate movement orders from your schedule and send one to each recipient in seconds.

04

Documents

One home for production paperwork. Sort it into categories, upload files, and search across everything.

05

Deliverables

Name, recipient, due date, status and approval for every item. Add technical specs. Apply a template for Streaming, Festival, Broadcast, Netflix, Prime Video, Hulu, Disney+ or Apple TV and get the whole package at once.

06

Wrap Production

A closeout checklist. Spend reconciled, no future shoot days, every deliverable signed off. It tells you what is left and where to fix it.

Connected

Call sheets pull from the schedule, the bookings and the locations. Wrap only clears when the budget is reconciled and deliverables are signed off.

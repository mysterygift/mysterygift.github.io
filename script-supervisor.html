SUPERVISOR

SUPERVISOR

Log the shoot as it happens.

Slates and takes against your shoot days, scene progress, the daily progress report and tramlines drawn on the script. Built to work on a tablet on set.

Download latest build See the features

Experimental. This is still being tested on set. Switch it on in Settings, under Developer, with Show experimental features. It may change between releases, and it stores its data on your computer only.

The short version

  • Slate, roll, cut, mark.
  • Tramlines drawn on the script.
  • Progress against the stripboard, every day.
  • Paperwork for the edit, ready to export.
01

UK or US slating

Choose once per production: consecutive numbers (217, 218) or scene and letter (23, 23A). The choice locks as soon as there is a slate, so a shoot never mixes systems.

02

Slate and take

New slate, and the camera, lens, stop, filter and rolls carry over from the last one. Roll take starts a stopwatch, Cut logs the take with its duration. Mark it Print, Hold or NG, with a reason for an NG, and add remarks. Keyboard shortcuts keep your hands on the job.

03

Line the script

Click the first and last line a shot covers, or drag down the lane. Click a segment to cycle on camera, off camera or not covered. Tramlines are labelled with the slate and shot type, and a strip flags any block covered by fewer than two. Undo the last 20 actions.

04

Script notes and photos

Add a note to any line: line change, ad-lib, cut, note, VFX, SFX or continuity. Tie it to a slate and its takes. Attach continuity photos to a take, tagged wardrobe, props, make-up, hair or set.

05

Progress, scene by scene

Pages shot, scenes and setups with takes, against the stripboard day by day. A table shows every scene's status, slates, takes and prints. Mark scenes complete or omitted, and enter screen time or a part-shot page credit.

06

Daily progress report

Record the day's actual times: unit call, first shot, lunch, back from lunch, first shot after lunch, camera wrap and unit wrap, plus remarks. Export the report as a PDF.

07

Paperwork for the edit

Continuity sheets as a PDF with one sheet per slate. The editor's log as a CSV with one row per take. A marked-up script PDF with the day's tramlines and notes. All filed in Documents.

08

A new script draft

Tramlines and notes are carried onto the new draft the next time you open the scene. Anything that cannot be placed is listed for you to line again, confirm or dismiss.

Connected

Shoot days come from the stripboard. A section shows Shot once its takes are printed. The unit call and wrap you enter are shared with Overtime on the People page.

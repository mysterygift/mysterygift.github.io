RELEASE FORMS

RELEASE FORMS

Signed on screen. Filed on the spot.

Bring up a contributor or location release, hand over the screen, and get the signed PDF filed in Documents.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Contributor and location releases.
  • Sign with a finger, Apple Pencil or mouse.
  • Signed PDF filed in Documents.
  • Your company name and your terms.
01

Two release forms

A contributor release and a location release. For a location, the address is required, and it and the shoot dates are written into the terms as you type.

02

Sign on screen

Hand over the device. The person reads the terms, signs in the box and types their full print name. On an iPad, Apple Pencil users can handwrite their name. Undo the last stroke, or clear and start again. The date and time are added at the moment of signing.

03

Under 18, and countersigning

For a contributor under 18, tick the box and a parent or guardian reads the consent and signs as well. On a location release, the producer can add a countersignature.

04

Filed and shareable

The signed PDF goes straight into Documents, under Releases, and you can save or share a copy. The Release Forms page lists every signed release for the production, ready to open, share or delete.

05

Your company, your terms

Enter your production company once, because the releases grant their rights to it. Edit the contributor, parent or guardian and location terms, and restore the standard wording whenever you like. A signed release keeps the exact terms it was signed under.

Connected

The company is the one printed on your call sheets, and a production with its own company grants its releases to that company. Signed releases are filed with the rest of the paperwork in Documents.

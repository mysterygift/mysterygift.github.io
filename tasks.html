TASKS

TASKS

Who does what by when.

Tasks for the whole production, from the first budget to the final master. Nothing gets lost between prep and wrap.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Assign by person or department.
  • Priority and due dates at a glance.
  • Templates for the jobs you always do.
  • Open items stay on your dashboard.
01

Task list

Description, notes, due date, department and priority. Break any task into subtasks and see their progress. Finished tasks fade back.

02

Search and filters

Filter by status, department, priority or due timing. Overdue and high priority tasks rise to the top.

03

Templates

Save a task list once and reuse it. Apply a template with an anchor date and every due date is worked out for you.

04

Reminders that create themselves

An invoice with a due date gets a task. A rented item with a return date gets a task. Mark the invoice paid or the item returned and the task completes itself.

05

Department responsibility

See open and overdue tasks by department, with the HOD responsible. Departments with open tasks and nobody in charge are flagged.

06

Your dashboard

Everything outstanding, in one place. Check it before you wrap and nothing is missed.

Connected

Invoices and equipment returns create their own tasks. Crew departments map to task departments. Outstanding tasks sit on your dashboard.

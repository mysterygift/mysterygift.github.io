BREAKDOWN

Tag it once. Every department gets its list.

Highlight words in the script, tag them by department, and track whether each one has been sourced. Hand every department a tick-box list.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Highlight a word, press a key.
  • Tags roll up into elements.
  • Sourced, in progress or needed.
  • A new draft? Your tags come with it.
01

Tag the script

Pick a scene, highlight words in the script, then click a category or press its key. There are eleven: cast, props, extras, costume, locations, lighting, foley and music, special FX, stunts and choreography, animals and children, and vehicles. Tag the same words in several scenes and they become one element with many tags.

02

Suggest cast and location

Albatross offers the scene's heading location and each speaking character. Untick any you do not want and add the rest as tags in one go.

03

The scene sheet

A breakdown sheet for each scene: scene details, page count, and a box per category listing its elements with a status dot. Export one scene as a PDF, or every scene, one page each.

04

Elements, kept tidy

See every element by category. Filter by category or status, or search. Rename an element, merge it into another, add notes for the department, or link it to a location, cast member, piece of equipment or music track.

05

Sourced, checked for you

Status is checked against the rest of the production. A location is sourced once it is booked or wrapped. A cast element is sourced when the person exists and is cast in every tagged scene. Lighting needs a matching item in Equipment. Music needs a matching track, and stays in progress until clearance is granted. Everything else you mark by hand.

06

Department lists

Export a tick-box list for each category, or for every department at once. They are filed in Documents with the script and sides.

07

A new draft

Import the new draft linked to the previous one and your tags are carried across, keeping their elements and statuses. A box tells you how many need a look, for example because the words moved or could not be found.

Connected

Locations come from your location list, cast from Cast Manager, lighting from Equipment and music from Music and Archive. A new script draft carries your tags across.

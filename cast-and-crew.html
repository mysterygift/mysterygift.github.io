CAST & CREW

CAST & CREW

Everyone on the production, one manager each.

Cast and crew each get a manager built for how that team works. It is the same people data as bookings, call sheets and tasks, so nothing is typed twice.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Cast and crew managed separately.
  • Crew grouped by department, HODs first.
  • Unavailable dates show up as clashes.
  • Departments with tasks and no HOD get flagged.
01

Cast Manager

Every cast member in one table: cast number, name, role, agent, contact and contributor form status. Search by name, role, cast number or agent. Filter by form status or by what is missing. Counts at the top show who has no cast number, role or agent details.

02

The details that matter

Identity, direct contact, agent and production admin in one form. Track each contributor form as Not requested, Requested, Signed or Expired, and tag the phases each person is working in.

03

Unavailable dates

Record single days or ranges when someone cannot work. If a cast member is scheduled on one of those days, it shows as a CLASH in Day Out of Days. Crew can have unavailable dates too.

04

Crew Manager

Crew sorted by department, heads of department first, then by role. Search by name, department, role, email or phone. Filter by department, HODs only, or anyone missing a department or role.

05

Set up in minutes

Open Crew Manager on a new production and Albatross offers a quick setup. Pick the departments you need and enter each Head of Department with their contact details. Skip it whenever you like and add crew by hand.

06

Your structure, your way

The departments and roles are set per production, under Crew structure in Settings. Add, rename and reorder departments and roles, or reset to the built-in default. Heads of department and task mappings come with it.

07

Wired into call sheets and tasks

Crew booked on a shoot day appear on the call sheet by department, HOD first. Crew Manager shows open and overdue tasks for each department, who is responsible, and which departments have tasks and no HOD.

Connected

Bookings use these lists. Call sheets group booked crew by department. Tasks follow departments to their heads.

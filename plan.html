PLAN

PLAN

Script in. Schedule out.

Import the script, break it into scenes, plan every shot and drag it onto the stripboard. When the plan changes, the rest of Albatross changes with it.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Script import turns pages into scenes.
  • Drag shots onto a stripboard of up to five units.
  • Locations, kit and risk assessments by shoot day.
  • Insights suggest where to group your shots.
01

Script Import

Paste your script, or upload a .txt or a PDF with a text layer. Albatross pulls out scene numbers, headings, INT/EXT and day/night. You check the scenes, then create them. Each scene is split into sections you can link to your shots, so you can see what you have covered.

The Script page →
02

Script Breakdown

Highlight words in the script and tag them by department: cast, props, costume, lighting, vehicles and more. Tags roll up into elements, and each element shows whether it has been sourced. Hand every department its own list.

The Script Breakdown page →
03

Shot Lists

Break every scene into shots. Size, lens, movement, support, cast and an estimated shoot time, all editable inline. Attach storyboard panels to the shots they belong to, and export the lot to PDF.

The Shot Lists page →
04

Stripboard

Unscheduled shots on the left, shoot days in the middle, the Boneyard on the right. Drag a shot onto a day, or assign a batch at once. Run up to five units a day, add Call, Lunch, Wrap and Move strips, lock a day when it is done, and export the shooting schedule to PDF. Scheduling Insights point out shots that share a support, size, location or cast but sit on different days.

05

Calendar

Every shoot day, every unit, on one month view. Drag a whole day to a new date, or just one unit, and its shots go with it. Drop a unit on another to swap Main and Second. If a date is taken, the two days swap. Days that run past 10h 30min get a warning.

06

Floor PlansExperimental

Draw a set or a unit base to scale, then plot the cameras, cast, lights and grip for every shot. Work out power, cables, truss and DMX, check the kit against Equipment, and print it.

The Floor Plans page →
07

Locations

Addresses, what3words, parking and access, fees, permits and release forms, and which locations are unbooked, on hold, booked or wrapped. Your Location Manager's best friend.

08

Equipment

Every item by department, with its source, rental dates and status. Tick several items and edit them together. Build a list for each shoot day, add kit from the registry in one go, tick items OUT and IN on set, and see what a list weighs against a van's payload. Export to PDF or CSV.

09

Risk Assessments

A risk assessment (RAMS) for every shoot day. Start from 17 built-in hazards, rate each one before and after controls, get it signed off, and export it to PDF.

The Risk Assessments page →
10

Script SupervisorExperimental

Slates and takes, tramlines drawn on the script, script notes with continuity photos, and the daily progress report. Built for a tablet on set.

The Script Supervisor page →

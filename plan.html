PLAN

PLAN

Script in. Schedule out.

Import the script, break it into scenes, plan every shot and drag it onto the stripboard. When the plan changes, the rest of Albatross changes with it.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Script import turns pages into scenes.
  • Drag shots onto a multi-unit stripboard.
  • Locations, kit and risk assessments by shoot day.
  • Smart Scheduler flags days you can tighten.
01

Script Import

Paste your script or upload a .txt or text-based PDF. Albatross pulls out scene numbers, headings, INT/EXT and day/night. You check the scenes, then create them. Each scene is split into sections you can link to your shots, so you can see what you have covered.

The Script page →
02

Shot Lists

Break every scene into shots. Size, lens, movement, support, cast and an estimated shoot time, all editable inline. Attach storyboard panels to the shots they belong to.

The Shot Lists page →
03

Stripboard

Unscheduled shots on the left, shoot days in the middle. Drag a shot onto a day. Run a Main Unit and a Second Unit side by side, with a column for each.

04

Calendar

Every shoot day, every unit, on one month view. Drag a day to a new date. If that date is taken, the two days swap. Days that run past 10h 30min get a warning.

05

Locations

Addresses, parking and access, permits and fees, and which locations are booked. Your Location Manager's best friend.

06

Equipment

Every item by department, with its source, rental dates and status. Build a list for each shoot day, tick items OUT and IN on set, export to PDF or CSV.

07

Risk Assessments

A risk assessment (RAMS) for every shoot day. Rate each hazard before and after controls, get it signed off, and export it to PDF.

The Risk Assessments page →
Connected

The stripboard is the source of truth. People checks bookings against it. Deliver builds call sheets from it.

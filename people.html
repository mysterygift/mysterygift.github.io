PEOPLE

PEOPLE

Know who is where.

Cast and crew in one place, booked against the schedule and checked against what each day actually needs.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Cast and crew managers in one place.
  • Bookings checked against the schedule.
  • Day Out of Days built from your bookings.
  • Gaps flagged before they cost you a shoot day.
01

Cast Manager

Add cast, assign roles such as character names, and keep agent and contact details in one list.

The Cast and Crew page →
02

Crew Manager

Crew grouped by department, heads of department first. Search by name, role, email or phone. Filter by department, HOD, or anyone missing a role.

The Cast and Crew page →
03

Bookings

See and manage who is assigned to which shoot days, on a calendar or as a list.

04

Booking gaps

Albatross compares who each scheduled scene or shot needs against who is booked. Needed but not booked. Booked but not needed. It advises, and never changes a booking itself.

05

Day Out of Days

A cast by shoot day matrix showing WORK, HOLD, OFF and CLASH. Export it to PDF or CSV.

06

One page per person

Scenes and shots they appear in, days they are booked, and what is missing. Everything about one person on a single screen.

Connected

Bookings are checked against the schedule. Call sheets list booked crew by department. Departments with open tasks and no HOD get flagged.

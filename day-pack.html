DAY PACK

DAY PACK

Everyone gets their own pack.

Email everyone called to a unit their own copy of the day's paperwork, each page watermarked with their name.

Download latest build See the features

Experimental. This is still being tested on set. Switch it on in Settings, under Developer, with Show experimental features. It may change between releases, and it stores its data on your computer only.

The short version

  • One pack per person, watermarked.
  • Eight kinds of document.
  • Drafts open with the files attached.
  • Nothing sends until you press Send.
01

Pick the documents

Call sheet, movement order, script sides, shooting schedule, shot list, risk assessments, storyboard and floor plans. Each line says where it comes from and whether it is ready. If something was saved before the day last changed it says Check, and if it is missing there is a button to go and make it.

02

Everyone who is called

The list is the cast and crew on the unit's call sheet. Untick anyone who should not get it. Crew booked to one unit only get that unit's pack. Leave cast agents in or out with one tick.

03

Your wording

Edit the subject and message, with each person's first name, name, the production, date, day and unit filled in for you. Your wording is kept for the production.

04

Check it, then send

Prepare the packs and each person's copies are made in their own folder. On a Mac with Apple Mail or Outlook, Open draft opens a message with the address, subject, message and files filled in. You check it and press Send. With other mail apps, the draft opens and the folder opens beside it so you can drag the files in.

05

A leak shows whose it was

Each person's name is printed faintly across every page of their copy. A page that gets out shows who it was sent to.

Connected

The pack is built from the call sheets, movement orders, sides, risk assessments, floor plans and storyboards you have already made. Bookings decide who is called.

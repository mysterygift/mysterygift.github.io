RISK

RISK

A risk assessment for every shoot day.

Build the RAMS, rate every hazard before and after controls, get it signed off and export it as a PDF. Your call sheet tells you if one is missing.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • One per shoot day, for one unit or both.
  • Every hazard rated before and after controls.
  • Signed off by a named person, with a date.
  • Missing or unsigned? The call sheet tells you.
01

Every shoot day, one list

See every risk assessment on the production. Sort by shoot day or location, filter by status or unit. Open, duplicate, export or delete from the row. Duplicate one onto other shoot days and each copy starts as a draft.

02

Hazards, built fast

Start blank, from a built-in hazard, or from hazards you saved earlier in this project. Name it, describe it, list the risks, outcomes and control measures, and mark who is at risk: crew, cast or public. Drag hazards into order.

03

Rate it before and after

Pick severity and probability, 1 to 5 each, on a 5 by 5 matrix. Albatross works out the factor and shows it in words as well as colour: Tolerable 1 to 6, Moderate 8 to 10, Severe 12 to 25. Rate it again once the controls are in.

04

Who to call

List the first aiders with phone and email, and add them from your crew in a click. Hospital and police details are filled in from the shoot day for you.

05

Signed off by a name

Approve needs at least one hazard and a responsible person. Albatross records who approved it and when. Change anything afterwards and it goes straight back to draft.

06

A PDF you can hand over

A4 landscape. A red DRAFT, NOT APPROVED banner until it is signed off, then a green banner with the approver and date. Each hazard shows both matrices with the ratings picked out. It is filed in Documents with the day's other paperwork.

07

The call sheet checks

Export a call sheet for a day with no risk assessment, or one that is not signed off, and Albatross warns you first. It is a warning, not a lock. You can export anyway.

Connected

A risk assessment belongs to a shoot day on the stripboard. Call sheets warn when one is missing or unsigned, and the PDF is filed with the day's paperwork in Documents.

Budget Schedule Crew Tracker

End-to-end production management for independent filmmakers who deliver.

Albatross is a free, open-source desktop app for production managers across indie and small-scale film, TV, and video work. Plan budgets, build schedules, track spend, and deliver confidently — all running locally on your machine.

Did I mention that it's free?

Features

Your entire production, at your fingertips.

We designed Albatross out of frustration with existing tools. We built Albatross to be: easy to use, and deeply interactive. Every feature in Albatross talks to each other, so when you're working in Albatross it's like working with your own personal production team.

It really is that easy.
Powerful Budgeting
Comprehensive smart budgeting tools

Build and manage budgets for productions at any scale. Albatross supports actualisation, fringes & contingencies, cost reports, currency switching, custom cost groups, vendor management, and exports cost reports with a click of a button.

POs, Invoices & Floats
You'll never miss an invoice again

We've built a powerful PO/invoice management system that integrates with your budget. Track purchase orders and invoices, reconcile expenditure, and manage floats — everyone loves it when production pays on time.

Industry-standard scheduling
What you need, when you need it

As you'd expect, Albatross allows you to manage each shoot day through a stripboard-style schedule. This pulls through your shots defined in your shot list automatically, allows you to generate call sheets, and supports episodic projects with multiple shooting blocs. You can even generate watermarked call sheets with the click of the button for all crew/cast assigned to a shoot day. It can even create movement orders!

Smart Scheduler
Your eye in the sky

Albatross analyses cast, crew, locations and equipment used on each shoot day and indentifies opportunities for you to streamline your schedule saving you and your production time and money.

Equipment & Kit Lists
No more gear left at the rental house

Albatross allows you to track all equipment associated with a project, by department, track where it's being sourced from, manages rental dates and produces kit lists for each shoot day on your project, empowering you to make sure no essential gear gets left on or off set.

Tasks
Keeping your team on the same page

Assign tasks by person or department, track priority and workload, and keep momentum from early budgeting through to delivering your masters.

So much more...

Get an at-a-glance view across your slate, share projects as compact .APF files, parse scripts into scenes, and track deliverables against templates aligned with major platforms. And you can even import storyboard PDFs from software like Athena Gallery, and parse them to build out your shot list.

Workflow

Pre-pro to delivery — connected.

Albatross is designed to give indepndent and established filmmakers a bespoke tool to manage their full production process.

Pre-Production
Budget | Shot List | Schedule

Build out your preliminary budget, create your shot list and then schedule it.

Principal Photography
Book Crew and Cast | Track Spend & Expenses | Instant Call Sheets and Movement Orders

Manage crew and cast bookings for your shoot days with our intuitive crew and cast managers that flag any bookings that have slipped through the net, manage and allocate receipts to budget codes, manage floats for each of your crew, and generate call sheets/movement orders with recipient names in seconds. Weather, directions, schedules and more are populated for you.

Post-Production
Deliverables | Wrapping Production

Clean master? Vertical and 16:9 cuts? Fifty different dubbed versions? Track them all with Albatross. And you can track all outstanding tasks on your dashboard, making sure nothing is missed when wrapping a production.

Albatross also runs locally, with support for a local server allowing the entire production office to collaborate free of charge.

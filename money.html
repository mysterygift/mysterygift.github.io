MONEY

MONEY

Every penny, accounted for.

Budget, spend and paperwork in one place. Know where you stand against budget today, not at wrap.

Download latest build See the features

The short version

  • Actuals against budget, as they happen.
  • POs, invoices and floats tied to budget codes.
  • Fringes and contingency built in.
  • Currency switching and one-click cost reports.
01

Budget

A full chart of accounts, rolled up. Budget, actual, variance and percentage spent for every account, with line items underneath. Fringes and contingency sit on top as percentage rules.

The Budget page →
02

Log Spend

Log spend by type: labour, purchase, rental, allowance or deposit. Each type has its own form. Save and add another. Anything uncoded waits in its own list until you recode it.

03

Cost Report

A cost report built from your live numbers. Export it as a PDF, or export the line items to CSV.

04

Vendors, POs and invoices

Track purchase orders and invoices per vendor. Raise or lower a PO at any time and every change is logged with a reason. Invoices run from draft to paid, and due dates create their own tasks.

05

Receipts and proof

Attach a receipt to any expense. Spend with no proof, or no PO, gets a badge so it does not slip through.

06

Floats

Allocate petty cash to a crew member and a line item. Reconcile it against their expenses when it comes back.

Connected

Invoice due dates create tasks. Rental equipment can be added straight from an invoice. Wrap checks that your spend is reconciled.
